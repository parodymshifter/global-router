Routing Difficulty Atlas

Pre-routing measurements of every global-routing benchmark this project routes, next to how hard each one turned out to be. Pick measures, see whether the designs cluster, and fit outcomes by least squares. Areas are in GCells (the routing grid's cells); "Steiner" is the rectilinear Steiner minimal tree (RSMT), the shortest right-angle tree joining a net's pins.

Any two measures

Each point is one design. The numbers under the chart are Spearman rank correlations (from -1 to 1; 0 means no ordering relation), for all designs and within each contest.

Do the designs cluster?

The selected measures are standardized (each to mean 0, standard deviation 1) and projected onto their two principal components: the two directions along which the designs differ most. Shaded outlines are k-means clusters, computed on all selected measures, not just the two plotted.

Which measures track which outcomes

Spearman rank correlation between each pre-routing measure and each routing outcome. Outcomes from different contests are not on one scale, so the contest filter matters: "All" pools ranks across contests, which mixes different scoring rules.

Least-squares fit

Ridge regression (least squares with a penalty that shrinks coefficients toward zero; penalty 0 is ordinary least squares) on standardized measures. The leave-one-out score refits the model once per design without it and predicts it: with this few designs, that number is the honest one. Keep the measures few.
